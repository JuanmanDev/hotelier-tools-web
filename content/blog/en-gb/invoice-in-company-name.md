To invoice a company instead of the guest, put the company's name, tax ID and address on the reservation **before** you generate the invoice. In Little Hotelier that means the **Primary contact** on the reservation's **Details** tab, the **ID number** field and **Organisation**. If the invoice already exists, edit the details and generate a corrected one. Hotelier Tools adds two shortcuts: a **Billing and address** editor on each reservation, and a private link where guests type their own company details.

## "In another name": to whom, or from whom?

Two different requests hide behind "an invoice in another name":

- **Made out TO someone else.** A company pays for its employee's stay, or a travel agency pays for its client. The invoice still comes from your property; only the customer block changes. This is the common case, and most of this guide.
- **Made out FROM someone else.** A different business issues the invoice: another company that runs some of your rooms, or the owner's own name for a block of stays. That changes your own header, not the customer's. It is rare, and covered at the end.

Never mix them up. The guest's company goes in the customer block, never in your header.

## What must a company invoice include?

In the EU, a full VAT invoice includes the issue date, a unique sequential number, your name and address and the customer's, the customer's VAT number when the customer is liable for the tax, a description of the service, the price before tax, the VAT rate and the VAT amount ([European Commission](https://taxation-customs.ec.europa.eu/taxation/vat/vat-businesses/invoicing_en)).

In Spain, the Tax Agency lists what a **factura completa** must carry ([AEAT: contenido de las facturas](https://sede.agenciatributaria.gob.es/Sede/iva/facturacion-registro/facturacion-iva/contenido-facturas.html)):

- number and, if you use one, series, with consecutive numbering
- issue date, and the date of the stay if it differs
- full name or company name, NIF and address of you **and** of the customer
- a description of the service, the unit price before tax and any discount
- the VAT rate and the VAT amount, shown separately

A **factura simplificada** leaves the customer's details out. For accommodation it is only allowed up to **400 euros including VAT**. The 3,000-euro limit applies to restaurant and bar services, not to rooms ([AEAT: facturas simplificadas](https://sede.agenciatributaria.gob.es/Sede/ayuda/manuales-videos-folletos/manuales-practicos/manual-iva-2026/capitulo-10-obligac-formales-suj-registro/obligaciones-materia-facturacion/facturas-simplificadas.html)). A business guest who wants to deduct the VAT needs the full invoice in the company's name, so ask for the company name, tax ID and address.

## How to put a company's name on a Little Hotelier invoice

Little Hotelier has no separate "bill to a company" switch. Its help centre describes this method, as of November 2026 ([Reservations FAQ](https://helpcentre.littlehotelier.com/en/articles/8674048-reservations-frequently-asked-questions)):

1. Open the reservation and go to the **Details** tab.
2. In **Primary contact**, type the company name. The invoice prints the name without the field labels.
3. Type the company's tax number in the **ID number** field. It prints just below the company name.
4. Add extra address details under **Organisation** if needed.
5. Keep the real guests in the **Guests** tab, as Little Hotelier recommends.
6. Go to **Invoices**, click **Generate invoice**, then **Email** > **Invoice** ([help centre](https://helpcentre.littlehotelier.com/en/articles/8673812-generate-print-and-email-a-guest-invoice)).

Your own details at the top (title, numbering, logo) are set once in **Customise invoices** ([help centre](https://helpcentre.littlehotelier.com/en/articles/8910582-customise-guest-invoices)).

**Already issued?** Edit the details, and the **Invoices** tab shows **Invoice is outdated**: click it to generate a corrected invoice. If you had marked it as final, cancel it and generate it again ([help centre](https://helpcentre.littlehotelier.com/en/articles/8672961-mark-reservation-invoices-as-final)). In Spain with VeriFactu, a finalised invoice is changed through **Rectify invoice** ([help centre](https://helpcentre.littlehotelier.com/en/articles/15220249-mark-an-invoice-as-final-spain-with-verifactu)).

### Booking.com guests and travel agencies

Booking.com says the property invoices the guest, in the guest's name or another name agreed with them, and never shows Booking.com as the seller ([Booking.com Partner Hub](https://partner.booking.com/en-gb/help/policies-payments/guest-payments/supplying-guests-invoices)). So a Booking.com guest can ask for their employer's name. A business traveller who presses **Request invoice** in Booking.com for Business simply sends you a message with the details ([Booking.com for Business help](https://help.business.booking.com/hc/en-gb/articles/14862570197268-How-can-I-get-my-invoice)).

For a travel agency that pays you directly, the agency is your customer: its name, tax ID and address go on the invoice, and the traveller's name can go in the description.

## How can guests send their invoice details themselves?

In Little Hotelier on its own, they can't. The guest writes to you, someone retypes the company details into the reservation, and the request gets lost if nobody follows it up before checkout. Little Hotelier's automatic guest emails don't ask for invoice details ([help centre](https://helpcentre.littlehotelier.com/en/articles/8910590-customise-guest-emails)).

## The faster way: billing details, guest links and resending in Hotelier Tools

Hotelier Tools works on top of your Little Hotelier account. These features are part of **Invoice on Leave**, which is in beta.

| Situation | Where in Hotelier Tools |
|---|---|
| You have the company details and want to type them | The reservation's **Guests** > **Billing and address** |
| The guest should send the details themselves | **Guest links** (`/invoice-on-leave/links`) |
| Every departing guest should be asked | **Guest link settings** > **Invite every guest** |
| The guest never got the invoice | **Send again** on the reservation, or **Send my invoice again** on the guest's link |
| The invoice must come from another business | **Invoices in another name** (`/invoices/issuers`) |

### Edit the billing details on the reservation

1. Open **Reservations** (`/reservations`) and click the stay.
2. In **Guests**, find **Billing and address** ("Who the invoice is made out to. Saved straight to Little Hotelier.") and click **Edit**.
3. Fill in **Company**, **Tax ID or document number**, **Address**, **City**, **State or province**, **Postcode** and **Country**, then **Save**.

The change is written to Little Hotelier's main guest record. If someone changed the reservation after you opened it, nothing is overwritten and you see what Little Hotelier holds now. A tax ID that doesn't look like one (8 to 16 letters and digits, with at least one digit) is refused. Once an invoice was issued, the company and tax ID can't be changed here: correct them in Little Hotelier and issue the invoice again.

### Send the guest a private link

A guest invoice link is a private page for one reservation. The guest sees what is on file (partly hidden), adds or corrects the company, tax ID and address, and can tick **Email me the invoice when I check out**.

1. Open **Guest link settings** (`/invoice-on-leave/links/settings`) and turn **Guest links** on.
2. Under **What guests may change**, set each field to not asked, optional or required. Email and phone are never editable: they decide who receives the invoice.
3. Under **Message for guests**, write the text at the top of the form, once as the default and, if you like, once per language.
4. Choose **How changes are applied**: **Fill blanks automatically** (new details go straight to Little Hotelier, changes to details on file wait for review) or **Review everything**.
5. On **Guest links** (`/invoice-on-leave/links`), press **New link**, find the booking, and choose **Create and copy link** or **Create and email to the guest**. The email goes to the address on the booking.

What the guest sends that changes something on file shows up under **Waiting for your review**, with **Approve and apply** or **Reject**. Before writing, Hotelier Tools reads the booking again, and a detail your staff changed after the link was sent always waits for a person. A guest who ticks the checkout box is enrolled in Invoice on Leave, so the invoice is generated and emailed after they leave.

A few safety rules are built in. A link shown once can't be shown again: lose it and you press **Get a new link**, which stops the old one. Links with more than one use always ask for the surname or booking reference first, and five wrong answers lock the link. A link works for at least 48 hours and at most 90 days.

### Invite every guest at checkout

Instead of sending links one by one, switch on **Invite every guest** in **Guest link settings**. Each day, at the hour you choose, every guest of the stays you pick gets their own link: **On the check-out day** (recommended), **The day before check-out**, **On the arrival day** or **Another day**. It goes by **Email** to the booking's address (Booking.com and Expedia forward it), by **WhatsApp** when a phone number has its country code, or both. Each stay is invited once.

**Skip stays already invoiced** is on by default. Turn it off and those guests are told their invoice was emailed, with a link to have it sent again. A **Reminder** can email guests who haven't filled in the form after 1 to 14 days; it sends a new link and the earlier one stops working.

### Send an issued invoice again

When a guest says the invoice never arrived, open the reservation's **Invoices** section and press **Send again**. Little Hotelier emails the same invoice to the address on the reservation. Nothing new is issued.

Guests can do it themselves too. With **Guests can ask for their invoice again** on (the default), the guest's page offers **Send my invoice again** once the stay is invoiced or over. It always goes to the booking's own address, at most 3 times, 12 hours apart, and the page never says whether an invoice exists.

### Booking.com invoice requests in the inbox

When a guest presses Booking.com's own invoice request, Booking.com sends a fixed message with the company name, tax number and "additional information". The Hotelier Tools inbox (alpha) recognises those two templates without using AI. Its analysis proposes the company details and the invoice at checkout on one card, with a reply in the guest's language. Nothing changes until you approve the card. See [Booking.com guest messages](/blog/booking-com-guest-messages).

### When the invoice must come from another business

If some stays must be invoiced **by** another company or the owner, use **Invoices in another name** (`/invoices/issuers`). It is off until you switch on **Invoice issuer per reservation** in **Settings > Invoices**. Then:

1. Create an issuer with **New issuer**: name, tax ID, address, phones, email, and optionally invoice title and logo.
2. On the reservation, under **Invoices**, choose **Invoice in another name…** and pick the issuer, or **Save and issue invoice**.
3. Hotelier Tools writes a tag in the reservation's notes, switches Little Hotelier's property details to that issuer for a few seconds, generates and emails the invoice, then puts your own details back.

Know the limits before you use it:

- **Issue these invoices from Hotelier Tools only.** Little Hotelier doesn't read the tag, so its own invoice button prints your property's details.
- **Every issuer shares your invoice numbering** in Little Hotelier. In Spain each issuer normally needs its own series. Check with your accountant first.
- **For a few seconds, Little Hotelier carries the other issuer.** An optional night run (recommended at 02:30) issues these invoices when nobody else is invoicing.
- An invoice already issued keeps its issuer. A correction is a credit note in Little Hotelier.

## Summary

- An invoice for a company keeps you as the seller and puts the company's name, tax ID and address in the customer block.
- In Little Hotelier: company name in **Primary contact**, tax number in **ID number**, then **Generate invoice**. Already issued? Generate a corrected one.
- In Spain, a business guest needs a **factura completa**; a simplified invoice for a room is only allowed up to 400 euros including VAT.
- Hotelier Tools lets you edit **Billing and address** on the reservation, send guests a private link to fill in their own details, invite every guest at checkout, and resend an issued invoice.
- Invoices issued **by** another business are a separate, opt-in feature with real limits: shared numbering, and Hotelier Tools only.
