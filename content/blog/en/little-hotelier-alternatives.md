For a small hotel, B&B or hostel, the closest Little Hotelier alternatives are **Cloudbeds** (all-in-one, priced by quote) and **Beds24** (pay per unit, very configurable), with **Mews** for larger hotels with a team. If your property is mostly apartments or vacation homes, look at **Lodgify**, **Smoobu** or **Hostaway**. All six publish an API, which Little Hotelier does not offer for your own reservations. Before you switch, export your history: Little Hotelier masks guest details 180 days after check-out.

Every price and feature below was checked on the vendors' own sites on **3 October 2026**. Prices change, so confirm before you sign.

## Is Little Hotelier part of SiteMinder, and who is it for?

Yes. Little Hotelier is SiteMinder's software for small properties: its website is published by SiteMinder Limited, and SiteMinder's [developer guide](https://developer.siteminder.com/smx-api/additional-resources/reference-tables/property-publishers) lists it among "SiteMinder's own products". Its channel manager is SiteMinder's.

According to its [pricing page](https://www.littlehotelier.com/pricing/), Little Hotelier specializes "in software for properties with 1-30 rooms". As of October 2026:

- **Basics:** channel manager, booking engine, calendar and mobile app, with a **1% booking fee** on top of the monthly price (their example: $1,000 of bookings in a month adds $10).
- **Pro:** everything in Basics plus competitor rate and rate parity insights, phone support and an onboarding specialist.
- **Add-ons:** Payments, Hotel Metasearch, Channels Plus, Dynamic Revenue Plus, Guest Engagement and Website Builder.
- **Price:** "from" a monthly amount that depends on your rooms and location; the page shows it once you enter your room count. There is a **30-day free trial**.

## What do Little Hotelier users like and complain about?

On [Capterra](https://www.capterra.com/p/144307/Little-Hotelier/reviews/), Little Hotelier scores **4.1 out of 5 from 236 reviews** (October 2026). Owners like having channel manager, calendar and booking engine in one place. The recurring complaints:

- the mobile app does less than the web app
- "the reports and syncing to accounting software can be improved"
- invoicing problems and some confirmation-email glitches
- support delays

Two structural limits matter if you plan to grow your tools around it:

- **No API key for your reservations or guests.** SiteMinder's APIs are for certified partners; properties can only generate a Direct Booking API key for rates and availability ([full answer](/blog/little-hotelier-api)).
- **Exports are manual,** and guest details are masked 180 days after check-out ([how to export in time](/blog/export-little-hotelier-reservations-excel)).

## Which alternative fits your property?

### Cloudbeds

The most direct swap for a hotel or hostel. Cloudbeds says it charges [no added commission](https://www.cloudbeds.com/pricing/) on its booking engine or channel manager, includes multi-property, payments, reporting and 24/7 support in every plan, and connects with "400+ integration partners via API". Pricing is by quote, so you cannot compare on paper without a call.

### Mews

Built for hotels that run more than rooms: restaurant, bookable services, a front-desk team. Mews is [priced per room](https://www.mews.com/en/pricing) (the figures it shows are for a 10-room property) and offers an open API with 1,000+ marketplace integrations. For a 6-room B&B it is likely more system than you need.

### Beds24

The budget, do-it-yourself option. Beds24 charges [from €15.50 a month](https://beds24.com/pricing.html) based on your units, plus €0.55 a month per channel connection, with no contract and no booking commissions. It is very flexible, which also means more setup work.

### Lodgify, Smoobu and Hostaway

These three are built for vacation rentals. They fit if your property is apartments or houses rather than hotel rooms.

- **Lodgify:** strong on direct-booking websites. Plans from Basic to Ultimate, a 0% booking fee and a [7-day free trial](https://www.lodgify.com/pricing/).
- **Smoobu:** simple and per unit, [from €28 a month](https://www.smoobu.com/en/pricing/) for one unit and from €9.60 per extra unit, with a 14-day free trial. Its Flex plan swaps the prepaid fee for a 0.9% booking fee.
- **Hostaway:** aimed at rental managers. The [pricing form](https://www.hostaway.com/pricing/) starts at 2–14 listings and ends in a quote.

## When Little Hotelier is still the right choice

Switching costs time: rebuilding rates, remapping every channel, retraining staff. Little Hotelier is still a sound choice when:

- you have **up to 30 rooms** and want one supplier for PMS, channel manager and booking engine
- you rely on **SiteMinder's channel manager** and add-ons such as Demand Plus or Hotel Metasearch
- you need **compliance features** it already has, such as [automated guest reporting](https://help-platform.littlehotelier.com/en/articles/10066500-automated-compliance-reporting) with Guest Engagement (Italy, Spain, Portugal, Germany, Switzerland, Austria, Croatia, the Czech Republic and Colombia) or VeriFactu in Spain
- your complaints are about **missing extras** (reports, invoices, checks, AI), not about the core calendar and channel sync

## What you lose when you switch

- **Guest history.** Guest details are masked [180 days after check-out](https://helpcentre.littlehotelier.com/en/articles/8673959-search-and-view-a-reservation), and the channel manager search only reaches 548 days back. Export reservations and guest lists first.
- **Phone numbers.** The reservations CSV leaves them out "for security reasons", so a bulk export won't carry them over.
- **Invoices.** Download every PDF you must keep. The newer platform's **Reports → Operational → Invoices** can download them as a ZIP; there is also [a guide to downloading all invoices at once](/blog/download-all-little-hotelier-invoices).
- **Set-up work.** Room types, rate plans, restrictions and channel mappings have to be rebuilt, and each OTA reconnected on a cut-over date.

## Summary

- **Hotel or hostel outgrowing Little Hotelier:** get quotes from Cloudbeds and, if you have staff and F&B, Mews.
- **Tight budget and time to configure:** Beds24.
- **Apartments or vacation homes:** Lodgify, Smoobu or Hostaway.
- **Happy with the core but missing extras:** stay, and add apps, scripts or an API layer.
- Whatever you choose, **export your data now**, before guest details are masked.
